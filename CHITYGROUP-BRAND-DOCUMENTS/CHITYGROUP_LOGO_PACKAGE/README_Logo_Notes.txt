CHITY GROUP LOGO PACKAGE  (v1.0, 2 October 2026)
=================================================
All logos are TRUE VECTORS (SVG) plus transparent PNGs, tightly cropped, long edge 3000 px.
Clear space is NOT baked into the files; apply it when placing the logo.

FOLDERS
01_Primary_Stacked      Mark above wordmark. With tagline, and "NoTagline" for small uses.
02_Secondary_Horizontal Mark left of wordmark. With tagline, and "NoTagline".
03_Submark              The C alone. FullColor, Navy / White (with fold line), NavySolid / WhiteSolid (no fold line).
04_AppIcon_Favicon      App icon 1024, favicon (SVG, PNG 32, ICO 16/32/48/64), Apple touch icon 180.

NAMING   CHITYGROUP_[Asset]_[Variant]_[Background]_[FORMAT].ext
Variant: FullColor | Navy | White (NavySolid, WhiteSolid for submarks)
Background: Light = use on light surfaces, Dark = use on dark surfaces, Transparent = works on either.

APPROVED COLOURS USED (nothing else appears in any file)
Deep Navy #0B1220 | Royal Blue #1E6BFF | Sky Blue #5AB8FF | Cloud White #F7F8FA | Action Blue #0D60FF | Fold Shade #143E90
Mark gradient: Royal Blue (bottom-left) to Sky Blue (top-right), 45 degrees. Fold band: Fold Shade to Royal Blue.
Tagline: Sky Blue on dark, Action Blue on light (passes contrast), same colour as the logo in one-colour versions.

USAGE RULES
Clear space: X on all four sides, where X = full height of the C symbol in the logo used.
Minimum width: stacked with tagline 200 px / 2 in; stacked or horizontal without tagline 120-140 px / 1.5 in; submark 24 px / 0.35 in.
Below the tagline minimum, use a NoTagline file. One-colour fold-line marks are for 80 px and larger; below that use the Solid submarks.
Never put the tagline on a gradient. Never recolour, outline, add shadows, or redraw.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE IS BASED ON / KNOWN LIMITS
- The C mark was traced from the highest-resolution version in your supplied lockup SVGs (a 1428 px image) and rebuilt as vector shapes.
  Match to your original silhouette: 99.2% overlap, mean edge deviation 1.1 px on a 1421 px mark (0.08%).
- Changed on purpose: gradient moved into the brand palette (the cyan is gone), glossy edge highlights removed, fold shading simplified,
  all variants now come from one master shape (the old files were three different drawings).
- Wordmark letterforms are your original outlines, unchanged. The word GROUP looks like a Light weight of Montserrat; that font file was not
  supplied, so it cannot be re-typeset. Do not retype the wordmark; use these files.
- Tagline is typeset from Inter Medium (your supplied font), caps, +0.1 em tracking, equal spacing around the bullets.
- Not included: CMYK/Pantone versions, EPS/AI files, a trademark or similar-mark search. The artwork originated from AI image generation:
  have an IP professional review before filing or launching.
- Your original supplied files were not modified.
